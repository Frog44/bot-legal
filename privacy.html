Introduction & Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how _________________ (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you interact with our Discord Bot (“the Service”, “the Bot”). By using this Bot, you consent to the practices described herein.

This policy applies to:
  • All members of Discord servers where this Bot is active.
  • Any interactions with this Bot through commands, reactions, buttons, or other features.

We are the data controller for information collected via the Bot. This policy does not govern your use of the Discord platform itself, which is governed by Discord's own Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect & Process

Solicited (You provide directly):

  • User ID: Your unique Discord numerical user identifier, required for Bot functionality.
  • Display Name & Avatar: When you use command features that display your identity (e.g., /profile).
  • Messages and Commands: Content you type into the Bot's commands, channels, or interactive elements.
  • Media Files: Images, videos, audio, documents uploaded via this Bot, if applicable.

Automatically Collected (We derive):

  • Server / Channel Information: Which server and channel the Bot interaction occurs in.
  • Usage Analytics: Command frequency, feature usage patterns, error logs, session metrics.
  • Webhook Data: Standard Discord webhook payloads including timestamps, event types, and action metadata.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • ❌ Private Direct Message content (except within server channels where the Bot is active).
  • ❌ Voice/audio data from voice channels unless you explicitly trigger a voice-related command.
  • ❌ Personal information without your consent or Discord's API scope authorization.

2. How We Use Your Information

  1. Service Functionality: To respond to your commands, deliver requested features, and enable interaction between Bot users and the Discord platform.
  2. Platform Compliance: To comply with Discord's API Terms of Service, Developer Agreement, and applicable law (including moderation logging where relevant).
  3. Moderation & Security: To prevent abuse, spam, rate-limit violations, and other threats to our server, your community, or other members.
  4. Analytics & Improvement: To analyze aggregate usage patterns, identify bugs, diagnose errors, and improve features (never using personally identifiable information in these analytics beyond what Discord's API provides).
  5. Legal Compliance: To respond to lawful requests, court orders, or government demands regarding illegal activity, data integrity, or copyright infringement.

No Sale of Personal Information

We do not sell, rent, lease, trade, or license your personal information to any third party for commercial purposes.

5. Data Retention & Security

Data Retention Policy

We retain Bot configuration data (channel lists, command settings, custom configurations) as long as the Bot is active in your server. When the Bot is removed or you request deletion:

  • Configuration Data: Retained for up to 30 days pending potential rollback or debugging.
  • Personal Identifiers (Discord IDs): Anonymized in logs within 14 days; fully deleted from operational databases within the same period unless retention is required by legal obligation.
  • Moderation Logs: Retained for up to 90 days by default (configurable). You may request early deletion at any time.

Security Measures

We employ commercially reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction, including:

  • 🔒 Encryption of data in transit via HTTPS/TLS and in storage using AES-256.
  • 🔒 Access controls limiting who can read user data (need-to-know basis). Data encryption and secure transmission protocols.
  • 📋 Regular security audits of our hosting infrastructure and application code.

Data Breach Notification

In the event of a confirmed data breach, we will notify affected users via Discord webhook / email within 72 hours of discovery, along with steps taken to mitigate consequences. This notification will comply with applicable law.
